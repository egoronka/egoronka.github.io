Survival Stories Uploader

A personal desktop tool that publishes the owner's own animated short videos ("what to do if…" survival stories) to the owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  • The owner renders short animated videos on their own computer.
  • The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app once.
  • The app uploads finished videos to the owner's TikTok account using the Content Posting API.

This tool is used only by its owner for their own account. It does not collect data from other TikTok users.
